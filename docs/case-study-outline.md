# Case study outline

The shared structure for every case study on the portfolio site, so all four read the same way. Content comes from `master_context.md` only (see the drafting guardrail in `rules.md`). Assets live in `case-study-assets/<project>/`; each section lists the ones that belong to it.

1. **Header:** project name, a one-line description, role and dates, a short stack line, and a live link if there is one.
2. **Summary:** two or three sentences covering what the product is, what I did, and where it ended up. Someone who reads only this should get the point.
3. **The problem:** what was wrong or missing, and why it mattered to the business or its users.
4. **My role:** what I owned, how the work started, and who I worked with.
5. **How I built it:** the main parts of the work and the hardest problems, told as decisions rather than a feature list.
6. **Outcome:** what changed. Use only facts that can be checked (shipped, who uses it, what it made possible). No made-up or estimated metrics.
7. **What I learned:** one or two honest lessons, including what I'd do differently.

**Length:** about 400–600 words of prose, with the screenshots and videos carrying the rest. Giving Gap, the largest project, runs longer (about 700). Written in first person.
